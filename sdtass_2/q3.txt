Q3: Difference between Data and Information (10 marks)

Data:
- Raw and unprocessed facts.
- No clear meaning by itself.
- Used as input.
- Example: 85, 90, 78

Information:
- Processed and organized data.
- Has context and meaning.
- Useful for decision making.
- Example: Average marks = 84.3

Key difference:
Data = raw facts
Information = meaningful output from data
